# SRD content — attribution and license

Adventura includes adapted rules text and spell metadata from the System Reference Documents published by Wizards of the Coast LLC. The included SRD material is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).

## D&D SRD 5.1 (2014 rules)

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The 2014 spell catalogue is stored in `src/data/spells-2014.json`. Each spell record identifies its edition with `contentSourceId: "srd-5.1"`. English structured spell data was transcribed in the 5e-bits/5e-database project (pinned revision: https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database/tree/536928f7460b5e5343dfb1805ded15706616765e). German structured spell text was transcribed in Heroquill from the official German SRD 5.1 translation: https://github.com/Mystikal91/heroquill. The official German document is available at https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.1/SRD_CC_v5.1_DE.pdf.

## D&D SRD 5.2.1 (2024 rules)

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The 2024 spell catalogue is stored in `src/data/spells-2024.json`. Each spell record identifies its edition with `contentSourceId: "srd-5.2.1"`. English structured spell data was transcribed in 5e-bits/5e-database (pinned revision: https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database/tree/b910e6981ce5de122738b331156954b6781b583e). German and English text was also checked against the bilingual transcription in Zauberkarten-Werkstatt (pinned revision: https://github.com/Lil-Dank/zauberkarten-werkstatt/tree/8b7d6fa43963315f14dbbd4f18a819a70bed6890). The official German SRD 5.2.1 document is available at https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/DE_SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf.

## Other SRD data

Class and subclass feature catalogues are maintained in `src/data/class-features-2014.json` and `src/data/class-features-2024.json`. Each record includes its own `contentSourceId`. The English and German feature texts are based on the respective official SRD editions. See the official SRD resources linked above.

## Changes

Adventura normalizes SRD material into its own JSON data model, stores English and German text together on canonical records, and derives structured damage summaries where present. These are adaptations of the source material. No endorsement by Wizards of the Coast is implied.
